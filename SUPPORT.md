# Support

## Questions and help with setup

Ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/innovayse/hostpanel/discussions/categories/q-a).
Answers there help the next person too.

For quick questions and chat, join the community on [Discord](https://discord.gg/NfqTkyTgD). Please check the [README](README.md) and the
`guides/` folder first.

## Reporting a bug or requesting a feature

Open an issue and pick the matching template:
[new issue](https://github.com/innovayse/hostpanel/issues/new/choose). Include the version or
commit, how you run HostPanel, and the steps to reproduce.

## Security issues

Do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report privately.

## Service level

HostPanel is free software provided by the community and its maintainers on a best-effort
basis. There is no paid support and no SLA.
